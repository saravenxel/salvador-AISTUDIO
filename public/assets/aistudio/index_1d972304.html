THE ATTORNEY

From Compton to
the courtroom.

Salvador Sánchez grew up in Compton. After earning his law degree at Rutgers in Newark and working in New Jersey, he returned to Southern California and opened his own law office in 2016.

Today, his Long Beach practice brings together criminal defense, immigration, and civil law. Clients can speak with him in English or Spanish.

HOW WE CAN HELP

A place to start.

01

Criminal defense

Discuss the charges, any upcoming court appearance, and what you want to understand about the process.

  • DUI and traffic-related criminal charges
  • Theft, assault, and other criminal charges
  • Juvenile matters and petitions for dismissal

For your consultation

If available, have your citation, hearing notice, and next court date handy.

02

Immigration

Talk through a family petition, a pending application, or a notice from an immigration agency.

  • Family petitions and permanent residence
  • Naturalization and citizenship
  • Asylum and removal defense

For your consultation

Have the most recent notice you received and any appointment dates available for the consultation.

03

Civil matters

Explain the dispute, who is involved, and what you would like to resolve.

  • Housing disputes and eviction matters
  • Debt collection and consumer rights
  • Vehicle accidents and personal injury

For your consultation

Set aside relevant letters or agreements and a short list of important dates.
